Understanding Autism

Why Is This Happening — And Why Your Child?

Autism shapes how a child communicates, connects, and experiences the world — things like eye contact, comfort in social settings, or the need for repeated routines. Modern science still doesn't fully know why it happens to a particular child, though genetics are understood to play a part.

Param Pujya Bhaiyyaji offers families another layer of understanding — a spiritual one. In his experience, a child's condition is often closely connected to the mother's own energy — emotions carried during pregnancy, and sometimes unresolved patterns from the family's ancestral line.

This is why, unlike centres that work with the child alone, Bhaiyyaji asks the mother to sit for healing too — because as she heals, her child is often seen to heal alongside her.

In your Day-One Session, Bhaiyyaji spends time with your family specifically — to hear your child's story, and share what he sees as the deeper cause behind it.

This spiritual guidance is offered alongside — never in place of — your child's medical, therapeutic, and educational care.

In this practice, every part of your child's experience is understood as energy — flowing freely, or gently asking to be unblocked. Here's how the seven chakras connect to what you see at home.

01
Muladhara — Root

Stability & Calm

Ruled by safety and grounding. An unsettled root often shows up as restlessness or hyperactivity.

02
Svadhisthana — Sacral

Flow & Adaptability

Governs ease with change. Balancing it can soften resistance to new routines, foods, or places.

03
Manipura — Solar Plexus

Willpower & Self-Regulation

The seat of self-control. Steadier behaviour and emotional balance are linked to this centre.

04
Anahata — Heart

Connection & Belonging

The centre of love. An open heart chakra is believed to support warmth and bonding with family.

05
Vishuddha — Throat

Expression & Communication

Governs the wish to be understood — including eye contact and reaching out to others.

06
Ajna — Third Eye

Inner Stillness & Rest

Associated with a quiet mind — supporting deeper, more peaceful, more settled sleep.

07
Sahasrara — Crown

Grace & Guidance

The doorway where Bhaiyyaji's mantras and blessings are believed to flow in during your sadhana.
