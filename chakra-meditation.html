What is Chakra Meditation?

Activate Your Energy Centres

The human body is made up of 7 primary chakras — the core energy centres that govern every aspect of our physical, emotional, mental and spiritual life. When these centres are blocked or unbalanced, disease, confusion and suffering follow.

PP Bhaiyaji's Chakra Meditation utilises mantra-based techniques — specifically calibrated for each chakra — unlike basic mindfulness. Participants report feeling a profound connection with themselves and the universe as each centre is awakened.

  • Emotional awareness, stability and regulation
  • Improved mental health and reduced anxiety
  • Enhanced mental focus and clarity
  • Stronger willpower and inner confidence
  • Boosted immune system and physical vitality
  • Spiritual growth and higher consciousness
  • Deep states of peace and inner silence
